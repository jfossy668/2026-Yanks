How It Works

Every remaining game receives a probability from team strength, Pythagorean record, opponent strength, location and recent performance. The projection sums those probabilities. Standings are grouped by AL East, AL Central and AL West, with Wild Card teams shown separately below the divisions. Clinch logic uses mathematical maximum/minimum finishes rather than guessing.
